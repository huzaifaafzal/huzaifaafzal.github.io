Case study / Reliability

Making recovery rehearsed, not theoretical.

At Harri, a workforce-management SaaS platform running across three AWS accounts and four environments, I organized disaster recovery work across the compute, network, and data layers, then ran drills so engineering teams could practice the procedures.

AWSTerraformOpenSearchElastiCache / RedisRDS / AuroraS3CloudWatchDatadog

Context

I worked with Harri through Kalam4Solutions as a contract DevOps engineer, supporting production EKS and ECS workloads and their dependencies: VPC networking, IAM, Aurora/RDS, Redis/ElastiCache, OpenSearch, DynamoDB, S3, and Lambda. The goal was to improve recovery readiness for the data layer across regions and give engineers procedures they could rehearse.

What I did

  • Used Terraform to support multi-region changes for the data layer, including OpenSearch, ElastiCache, RDS/Aurora, and S3.
  • Implemented OpenSearch cross-cluster replication.
  • Contributed to Aurora Global Database planning and multi-region recovery procedures, and supported S3 replication.
  • Supported Redis v7 upgrades, the move to the T4g instance family, and RDS/Aurora upgrades and maintenance, validating service behavior after each change.
  • Ran disaster recovery drills with engineering teams to rehearse responses to failure scenarios.
  • Documented change procedures, recovery checks, and runbooks, and reviewed AWS Well-Architected guidance and Trusted Advisor fault-tolerance findings.

Why drills, not just replication

Replication copies data. It doesn’t tell anyone what to do at 3 a.m. when a region has a problem. The drills turned documented procedures into steps engineers had actually practiced, and the recovery checks gave them a way to confirm a service was healthy afterwards.

Outcome

  • OpenSearch cross-cluster replication in place, with S3 replication and Aurora multi-region planning alongside it.
  • Engineering teams rehearsed recovery procedures through drills, backed by documented runbooks and recovery checks.
  • Datastore upgrades shipped with post-change validation.
